# ⚡ EV Market Performance & Insights

## 📌 Project Overview
This project explores the electric vehicle (EV) market through interactive Tableau dashboards. It analyzes EV categories, manufacturers, driving range, pricing, battery technologies, customer ratings, vehicle efficiency, and annual sales trends to help users understand key patterns in the EV market.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze the distribution of EV models across categories and manufacturers.
- Compare driving range, pricing, and battery capacity.
- Explore customer ratings and energy efficiency across EV categories.
- Examine annual EV sales trends by model year.
- Understand the distribution of EV models by battery type and manufacturing country.

## 🗂️ Dataset Overview
The dataset contains EV-related attributes such as:
- Vehicle model and manufacturer
- Model year and manufacturing country
- Battery capacity, battery type, and degradation
- Driving range and energy efficiency
- Price, insurance cost, and resale value
- Customer ratings and safety ratings
- Annual sales and estimated CO₂ savings

*Note: The KPIs and charts describe the dataset used for this project. The manufacturer and battery-type charts show model counts, not verified real-world market share. Annual sales trends use the sales values available in the dataset.*
